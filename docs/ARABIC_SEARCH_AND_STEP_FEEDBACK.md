# Arabic discovery, step feedback and independent answer sharing — 2026-11-05

Baseline: 9a86bb0 on codex/code-quality-review. Implemented in the standalone guide only.

Arabic search is deterministic and local. It normalizes Arabic marks/letters, ignores a bounded set of question filler words, recognizes curated synonyms, and accepts at most one edit for Arabic words of at least five letters. Codes/numbers do not use fuzzy matching. All meaningful query terms must match. Exact matches rank above synonyms and spelling approximations in customer and team libraries. Scope and publication filters remain authoritative. This is lexical assistance, not semantic AI or a guarantee of correct intent.

The editor suggests up to five similar titles from the authorized team catalog (up to its existing 1000-summary API limit), including drafts. Suggestions use token overlap and the same synonym groups; they never prevent saving or merge content. Failure to load candidates is visible. Existing published-only related-content choices remain separate.

Each customer step offers an optional needs-clarification comment. The feedback API rechecks article access, reply grant, valid step and exact published revision. It stores step number, title snapshot and article revision alongside normal feedback. A new additive migration extends the daily unique key to article/visitor/day/step/revision; overall feedback remains step/revision zero and existing rows retain those defaults. Different steps may receive separate comments; the same step/revision is limited to one daily response. Existing CSRF, rate limit and visitor hashing remain. Team feedback lists show the historical step/revision; existing counts now count feedback submissions including step responses, not unique customers.

Answer sending remains independent of training guides. Team share dialogs now read published articles through an authenticated endpoint, avoiding interference from a previously opened customer reply cookie. A team-only article view provides a direct answer-share panel with response/link copy, link-only copy, WhatsApp launch and singleton preview. Customer copies preserve their original scope. No messages are sent automatically.
